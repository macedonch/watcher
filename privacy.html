Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from Google. This is used solely to display your profile on Watcher.

How we use your data

  • Your display name and username are shown publicly on comments you post.
  • Your email is stored securely and never shared with third parties.
  • Watch history, liked videos, and Watch Later lists are stored locally on your device.
  • Comments and likes on videos are stored in our database (Firebase) so other users can see them.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase — authentication and database. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • YouTube — video content is loaded via YouTube embed player. Google Privacy Policy

Cookies and local storage

We use browser localStorage to save your preferences, watch history, and Watch Later list. We do not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies.

Data deletion

You can sign out at any time from your profile page. To delete all your data, contact us and we will remove your account from our database. Clearing your browser data will remove all locally stored information.

Children

Watcher is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the site administrator through the GitHub repository.
