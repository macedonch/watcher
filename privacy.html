Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. What data we collect

When using Watcher we may collect: your name and email (via Google Sign-In), profile data (avatar, banner, description), watch history, comments and messages.

2. How we use data

Collected data is used to: provide the service, personalize video recommendations, and improve quality of the platform.

3. Data storage

Data is stored in Firebase (Google Cloud) and in your browser's localStorage. We do not sell or share personal data with third parties.

4. Cookies and localStorage

Watcher uses localStorage to store settings, watch history, and authentication. We do not use advertising cookies or track users across other websites.

5. Google Sign-In

When signing in with Google we receive: your name, email, and profile photo. We do not access your Google password, contacts, or any other account data.

6. Data deletion

You can request deletion of your data by contacting macedonch@gmail.com. Local data (history, settings) can be cleared through your browser settings.

7. Contact

For privacy questions: macedonch@gmail.com
